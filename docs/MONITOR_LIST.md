# Monitor list — who @thenumberdesk watches, and why

**Created Sep 2, 2026** (marketing plan §4.3; directive §6). This is the fuel
for the reply engine. Replies under big posts are the only thing that reaches
strangers at our size, so the hourly X task works from THIS list, not from
whatever the algorithm happens to show it.

**How the X task uses it.** Every round: open the "Watch" columns in the order
below, take the posts under 45 minutes old with real traction (5K+ views, or
any post from a Tier 1 account), and answer with the number — what the market
says about the thing they just said. Never a pitch, never a link in a reply.
One reply per account per day; two per thread at most. Tier 1 = check every
round. Tier 2 = check twice a day. Tier 3 = check when its sport is in play.

**Handle hygiene.** Accounts marked ✓ were confirmed either by being on our
following list on Sep 2 or by having been replied to and verified live.
Everything else was written from knowledge and MUST be confirmed on first
use (open the profile, check the blue check / follower count, confirm it is
the real one, then mark ✓ here). Do not reply to a parody thinking it is the
source. When an account is dead, renamed or suspended, strike it here the
same day.

Counts: **~230 accounts** across 12 buckets. Target per the directive is
200-500; this grows by whatever the reply rounds find getting traction.

## Rules that travel with this list

1. Reply to the post, not the person. Bring the number. No link.
2. One reply per account per day. Two per thread max. Never a reply chain
   with ourselves.
3. If the post is a bad beat or a loss, punch up at the book or the price —
   never at the bettor.
4. If we cannot verify a handle is the real account, we do not reply to it.
5. Every reply that clears 1K views gets its account promoted a tier here.
