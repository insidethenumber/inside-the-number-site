**Internal links** → `/ufc-331-odds` · `/no-vig-calculator` · `/odds-converter` · `/glossary`

## Publishing discipline

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| One article per day, maximum | Seven thin pages is worse than three good ones |
| Never publish two on the same intent | They compete with each other, not with anyone else |
| Every article links to **≥3 existing pages** | The link-equity problem this sprint fixed must not come back |
| Every article has a snippet answer in the **first 60 words** | It is the only part most searchers read |
| Dated sections are labelled with the date they were counted | So a stale count is visibly stale, not quietly wrong |
| No article ships without its facts re-checked that morning | Every number above has an expiry |
