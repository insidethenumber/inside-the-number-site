### The pipeline, and the one non-obvious part

Chrome's download of a Gemini image was blocked/cancelled — the temp file
appeared in ~/Downloads and vanished. The route that works:
`computer {action:"zoom", save_to_disk:true}` over the image region, which
writes a readable PNG to the outputs folder. ~/Downloads is now also a
connected folder, so ordinary downloads are readable too.

Three heroes generated and cropped into `assets/heroes/`: `stadium-teal`,
`money`, `stadium-purple`. Prompts explicitly exclude text, logos, faces,
jerseys and readable signage — so no likeness or trademark exposure — and
reserve dead space for the caption.

## Bugs found and fixed today

1. `fill=None` on a Pillow rectangle draws an **outline** — a stray seam was
   crossing the price card.
2. Long team names ran into the price in the stack ("KENNESAW STKENN -22.5").
   Now the price reserves its width first and the name fits into what's left.
3. `split` and `left` layouts overflowed the canvas with a two-line headline
   plus a big number. Both now budget the vertical space instead of assuming.
4. Hero backgrounds were mis-mapped (a money photo under a "stadium" caption)
   because the source captures were selected by modification time and one was
   a duplicate. Now mapped explicitly.
5. A mouse cursor was baked into one capture. Cropped out.
6. ESPN's team endpoint and its scoreboard disagree on abbreviations (Buffalo
   BUF vs BUFF, UAlbany UALB vs ALB) — cards were silently drawing colour
   bars instead of logos. Fallback added. West Georgia and Merrimack are
   still genuinely absent from the cache.
