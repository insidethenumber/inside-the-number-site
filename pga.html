Add up the raw implied win chance of every golfer in the field and it does not land on 100% — it lands on 147%. That extra 47 points is the book's built-in edge, sitting in the price before a single tee shot is struck. It is bigger here than in most outright markets because a 128-man field means 128 separate cuts stacked on top of each other. Raw implied = 100 ÷ (100 + price), what the price alone says the win chance is. Fair = that number divided by the field's total (147%), so the whole field's fair chances add to 100%. Jackson Koivun is the favorite at +1325 (7.02% raw, 4.79% fair) — even the favorite is not expected to win four times out of five, or really at all most weeks.

How to read this page. Win chance is the market's honest opinion of each player once the bookmaker's cut is taken out — the whole field then adds up to 100%. Betting a golfer to win outright is the longest of long games: even the favorite misses far more often than he hits. Fun-sized stakes only.
