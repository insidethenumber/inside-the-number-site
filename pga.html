What the board is really telling you. Add up the win chances above and you get 52.5% — from fifteen players, in a field of roughly a hundred and fifty. A fair book adds to 100%. Everything over that is the house’s cut, and on a golf outright market it is the widest cut in mainstream betting: fifteen names here already account for more than half of a number that should cover the entire field. That is not a knock on these prices, it is what an outright market is. It is also why the honest version of “who wins” is 7.0% for the favourite — Jackson Koivun misses better than nine times out of ten.

How to read this. Win chance is the market's honest opinion of each player once the bookmaker's cut is taken out — the whole field adds up to 100%. Betting a golfer to win outright is the longest of long games: even the favorite misses four times out of five. Fun-sized stakes only.
