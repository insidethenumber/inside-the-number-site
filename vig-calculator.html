What the hold actually is

Convert both sides of a market to implied probability and add them up. A fair market would total 100%. A real one totals more — usually 104-105% on a two-way line. That surplus is the hold, also called the vig or juice: the sportsbook's fee, priced into the odds rather than charged as a commission.

Why a point or two matters more than it sounds

At -110 on both sides the hold is 4.76%, and you need to win 52.4% of your bets to break even. Find the same game at -105 and the hold drops to 2.44% and break-even falls to 51.2%. That 1.2-point difference is larger than the edge most bettors ever manage to find through handicapping. Paying less is the most reliable edge available.
