What the market is actually saying
No-vig true price · key numbers · line movement

A posted line is two things at once: an estimate of the outcome, and the price you are charged for taking it. Strip the vig out and what is left is the market's true probability — the number worth comparing anything else against. In football the key numbers carry most of the weight: games land on 3 and 7 far more often than on any other margin, so a spread moving off 3 costs more than the half-point makes it look. And when a total moves while the spread sits still, the market is repricing how the game gets played, not who wins it. Every current price, and how far each has traveled since it opened, is on the Full Board.
