Sun, Aug 30
MLB
Phillies @ Angels
THE PICK: Under 7.5 (-103) · 4:07 PM ET

Zack Wheeler pitches for Philadelphia in Anaheim against Yusei Kikuchi and a 52-84 Angels team. The interesting number is not the moneyline. This total opened at 8.5 and now sits at 7.5, a full run down — and on a fourteen-game board it is the only total that moved a full run today. Everything else that moved, moved half. So the market did real work on this game overnight. Here is the part that does not fit. Cutting a run out of a game should help the underdog: fewer runs means fewer innings where the better team can pull away, and a favorite’s price normally comes in when a total drops. Philadelphia’s did not. Strip out what the book keeps and the Phillies were a 66.6% side at open and are a 65.2% side now — essentially unchanged, while the run environment underneath them fell by a full run. One of those two numbers is stale. Then the price itself. After a move that big, the side the market moved toward is usually the expensive one. Here the under is the cheaper half of the game at -103 while the over is laid at -116. You are getting the direction the market just moved in, at the better of the two prices, on the lowest total on today’s board. The case against: Wheeler rarely goes past six, and the Anaheim bullpen behind him is where this profile breaks — a four-run seventh inning does not care what the opening number was. Kikuchi is also capable of walking his way into a big inning of his own. High confidence, not certainty. Odds via DraftKings, read at 8:15 AM CT today — check the number before you take it.

The Philosophy

Built on data.
Not opinions.

Inside the Number was built on a simple premise: most bettors lose because they react to narratives instead of numbers. The media tells a story. The public bets the story. The line moves. And sharp money fades the public. Our small team tracks that gap every day, across eight sports, so you don't have to.

Every pick we publish is backed by a documented process — historical ATS trends, line movement tracking, situational factors, and a consistent model. We're not guessing. We're finding spots where the number tells a different story than the headline.

The market data is free and always will be — the line, where it moved, and the true price underneath it. That much you can check for yourself. What we sell is the read on top of it: the projection, the edge, and the bet.
