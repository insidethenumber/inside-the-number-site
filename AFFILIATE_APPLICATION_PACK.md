## ⚠️ READ THIS FIRST — the $30,000 rule

Tennessee treats affiliates differently depending on **how you get paid**, and this is the single most important decision in the whole process:

| Deal type | What it means | TN requirement |
|---|---|---|
| **CPA** (flat fee per referred depositor) | You get e.g. $150 once, per new depositor | **No license required. $0.** |
| **Revenue share** | You get a % of the operator's adjusted gross income from that player, ongoing | Counts as a registered **Vendor** — **$30,000 registration fee** ($10k within 10 days of approval, $10k at year one, $10k at year two). Non-refundable. |

**So: accept CPA only. Decline revenue share and hybrid deals.** If an affiliate manager offers you "35% rev share, better long-term," the honest answer is that it costs $30,000 to accept legally in Tennessee. Say you're CPA-only.

This is my read of the TN Sports Wagering Council rule (SWC Rule 1350-01-.04(2)(g)(iv), which names "services in which the Vendor receives payment or compensation based on a revenue sharing agreement"). I'm not a lawyer — before signing anything rev-share, confirm with the SWC directly (Grant Crosthwaite, SWC CFO, 615-906-1563) or a gaming attorney. For CPA, multiple industry sources agree no TN license is needed.
