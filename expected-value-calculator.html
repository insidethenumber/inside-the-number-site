Expected Value Calculator

What a bet is worth on average if you could make it over and over. This is the number that actually determines whether a bet is worth making — not whether it wins.

Expected value per bet

The math is easy. The probability is the hard part.

EV calculation itself is simple arithmetic. What actually separates a winning bettor from a losing one is the input you can't look up: an honest estimate of how often the bet actually hits. Overestimate your own win rate by even a few points and a bet that looks positive-EV on this calculator is actually negative in reality.

A more reliable starting point than guessing is comparing the posted price to the no-vig fair price — the market's own opinion with the book's margin removed. If you don't have a specific reason to disagree with the market, that fair price is a better probability estimate than most people's gut feel.
