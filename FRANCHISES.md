# ITN franchises — the recurring formats for @thenumberdesk

**Created Sep 2, 2026.** The account had 372 posts and 31 followers. The
diagnosis, and it is correct: 372 one-off posts instead of a handful of
formats people learn to recognise. Franchises fix that. Someone should be
able to scroll past and know it is us before they read the handle.

Every post from here is one of these five. If it is not one of these, it is
a reply — replies stay free-form, that is where the conversation is.

---

## The honesty rule that shapes all of them

We publish what the **market** says, not what a model of ours says.

Our "model" de-vigs DraftKings against itself, so it structurally cannot
find an edge against DraftKings, and the private record through Sep 2 is
12-18. So there is no ITN EDGE franchise, and there never will be until
there is a real edge to report. Any format of the shape
"market 47% / our model 61% / 14% edge" is fabricated and banned.

Likewise **no ticket/money splits.** We do not have that data. It belongs to
Action Network and their peers. Inventing it is the fastest way to get
publicly corrected and lose the only asset we have.

What we DO have, honestly, and nobody else frames this well:
- the posted price on every game across eight leagues
- **open AND close** on moneyline, spread and total (ESPN's nested odds)
- the de-vigged true price and the break-even
- our own stated side, before kickoff, with the reasoning shown

That is enough for four data franchises and one joke.

## 1. THE BOARD — daily, morning

The day's slate, sorted by how close the market thinks each game is, with
the outlier called out. Card format: `split` or `board`.

Why it works: it is a service. It answers "what is on today and what is
worth my attention" in one image.

> THE BOARD — THURSDAY
> 11 games. 10 of them are 18.5-point favourites or worse.
> One is a football game.

## 2. THE NUMBER — daily

One number, as large as it will go, with the context under it. Card format:
`bignumber`.

Why it works: it is the tagline made literal. THE NUMBER DOESN'T LIE.

> THE NUMBER: −100000
> Utah's moneyline tonight. A hundred grand to win a dollar.

## 3. MARKET MOVERS — daily when something actually moved

Open versus current, and what the move implies. **We genuinely have this** —
ESPN's nested odds block carries `open` and `close` for every market, which
is why this franchise is real and not invented.

Rules: report the move, then say what it means. Never claim to know WHO
moved it — we cannot see that. "The number moved" is honest; "sharps moved
it" is not.

> MARKET MOVERS
> Colorado / Georgia Tech opened GT −7. It is −6.5 now.
> Half a point toward the dog on a game nobody is talking about.

## 4. THE PRICE IS THE POINT — 3-4x a week

The de-vig, explained on one game. This is the actual differentiator and it
is completely honest: we are not claiming an edge, we are showing what the
posted price means once the book's cut comes out.

> −299 and +238 together imply 104.5%.
> The 4.5% is the house. Strip it and the market says Dodgers 72%.
> That is what you are actually buying.

## 5. FOURTH QUARTER — daily, no numbers required

The joke. The relatable one. This is the franchise that makes the account
human, and it is the one the analysts skip and then wonder why nobody
follows them.

Register: the shared experience of betting. Punch up at books, prices,
touts and TV narratives. Never down at fans, players having a bad night, or
anyone who lost a bet. Memes, found video and plain funny observations all
qualify. A number is optional here and often better left out.

> college football is back tomorrow which means it is time to remember
> you're bad at this and do it anyway

| Slot | Franchise |

| Morning | THE BOARD |

| Evening | FOURTH QUARTER |
| 3-4x/week, floating | THE PRICE IS THE POINT |

Three originals a day maximum. Everything else is replies — a reply is worth
roughly 150x a like at our size, and replies are how strangers find us.

## What every franchise post carries

- an image (see `scripts/cards.py`), always, no exceptions on originals
- one or two lines of copy, maximum — the graphic carries the data
- the franchise name in the graphic so the format becomes recognisable
- the ITN mark and insidethenumber.com in the footer, so reposts carry it free
