1 · THE LEAD SITUATION

Falcons at Packers — the total (43.5 (-115), opened 46.5), Thu 7:15 PM CT, Lambeau Field, Prime Video.

Green Bay opened -7.5 and sits -7. That is half a point, and the moneyline barely blinked either — -360 to -340. The market has not really changed its mind about who wins this game.

The total is a different story. It opened 46.5 and is 43.5 now. Three full points came off the number while the spread moved half of one.

That combination has a specific meaning. A spread that holds while a total collapses is the market repricing how the game gets played — pace, possessions, scoring — not who comes out ahead. Those are two separate opinions and they moved independently this week.

The second thing worth noticing is where the spread stopped. It walked down onto 7 and stayed there, and the price moved from -110 to -105 instead of the number going to 6.5. Seven is the second most common margin in football. Books would rather charge you differently at 7 than move off it.

What would change it: Lambeau weather, listed at 58 degrees and intermittent clouds, and anything that lands on either quarterback in Thursday's report.

ALSO ON THE BOARD

Jets at Lions — the mirror image (total 45.5, opened 44.5), Sun, Detroit. Detroit is -9.5 and has not moved a tick since it opened. The total went the other way, up a full point. Same structure as the Packers game, pointed the opposite direction: equally sure about the winner, now expecting more scoring rather than less.

Titans at Giants — the number that would not move (NYG -3 (-120)), Sun, East Rutherford. The Giants opened -3 and are still -3. Nothing moved. But the price is -120 while the other side is +100. Three is the most expensive number in football to cross, so when a book wants a different balance there it changes what you pay rather than what you get.

Panthers at Browns — both markets agreed (CAR -2.5 (-108), total 40.5), Sun, Cleveland. Carolina moved from -1.5 to -2.5 and the total climbed 39.5 to 40.5. Side and total drifting together is the least ambiguous kind of move, and 40.5 is the lowest total on the Week 3 board.
