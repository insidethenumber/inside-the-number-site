1 · THE FREE PICK

Denver Broncos @ Kansas City Chiefs — Over 43.5 (-108), 8:15 PM ET, Arrowhead Stadium.

Monday night is the whole football board, so this is the only NFL number in play tonight. The total opened 42.5 and sits at 43.5 now — a full point up — while the spread hasn't moved an inch: Kansas City opened -2.5 and is still -2.5.

That split is the read. When a total moves and the side stays put, the market is repricing how the game gets played, not who wins it — and a full point on a Monday-night number, with plenty of volume behind it, is real money, not noise.

What beats it: 43.5 is already a low number for two offenses the market is clearly watching closely, and a divisional-style slog of field goals and stalled drives buries this without anything strange happening at all.

4 · WHAT THE MARKET KNOWS

Two ways a line can move, and both happened on tonight's board. Denver-Kansas City's total climbed a full point while the price on each side barely moved — that's the market repricing the game itself. Chicago-Cleveland's spread never left +1.5, but Cleveland's moneyline price grew by nineteen cents — that's money finding a side without moving the number at all. Knowing which kind of move you're looking at matters more than knowing which direction it went.
