1 · THE LEAD SITUATION

Liberty at Coastal Carolina — spread (LIB -2.5 (-112)), Thu 7:30 PM ET, ESPN.

Coastal Carolina opened as a 1.5-point home favorite. The number is now Liberty -2.5. That is a four-point move, and it crossed zero: the market changed which team it expects to win, not just by how much.

The total moved with it, from 54.5 to 50.5. A spread moving toward the road team while the total drops four points is the market repricing the whole game as lower scoring and less favorable to the home side at the same time.

Liberty -2.5 at -112 needs to cover 52.8% of the time to break even. With the book's share removed, the fair number is 50.4% — the two sides are priced close to even at the new number.

The moneyline tells the same story in different units: Liberty went from +100 to -135, a 57.4% break-even and 55.1% fair.

What would change it: an injury or starting-quarterback report before Thursday. A spread that has already moved four points has less room left in it than one that has not moved.
