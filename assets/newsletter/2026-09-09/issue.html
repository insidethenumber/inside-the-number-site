1 · THE FREE PICK

New England +3 vs Seahawks (+3 (-108/-112 total 44.5)), 8:20 PM ET.

Week 1 opens tonight in Seattle. New England opened +3.5 with a +160 moneyline; both have come in, to +3 and +142. So the market spent the week getting more confident about who wins. What it did not do is change its mind about the game itself: the total opened 44.5 and is still 44.5, not a half point either way.

Taking the Patriots means buying the key number. Three is the most common margin in football, and at +3 a late field goal still pushes rather than beats you. Strip out what the book keeps and the market makes Seattle about 60%, New England about 40%.

What beats it: Seattle winning by a touchdown, which is exactly what the market is drifting toward.

OTHER CONFIDENT PLAYS

Under 9, Blue Jays at Athletics (-105), 3:05 PM ET. The total opened at 9.5 and is 9. Toronto firmed at the same time, -185 to -193. The number came down while the market got more confident in the favorite, which is the market saying it expects Toronto to win without a shootout to do it. What beats it: the Coliseum playing small and an early bullpen game.

Detroit -135, Twins at Tigers (-135), 1:10 PM ET. Detroit opened -131 and is -135. The total has not moved off 8.5 at all. This is the flattest game on the board, which is its own kind of information: the market saw nothing all week worth changing its mind about. What beats it: a day game after a night game and a lineup with regulars resting.

2 · BIGGEST OVERNIGHT MOVE

Washington at San Diego was not close to a pick, but it moved more than anything else on the board. The Padres opened -199 and are down to -173; Washington's price eased from +163 to +142 with it. The spread never budged off 1.5. That is a price move with no line move behind it — the market got less afraid of the Nationals without changing how many runs it thinks separate the teams.

3 · THE NUMBER TO KNOW

26 — Cents the Padres' moneyline moved overnight — the largest single-game price move on today's board, bigger than either pick.

4 · WHAT THE MARKET KNOWS

New England's moneyline moved 18 cents overnight (+160 to +142) for a half-point spread move. Contrast that with Washington-San Diego above: 26 cents of price movement and the spread never moved at all. Same market, two different things being priced — one bet got more confident about the margin, the other only got more confident about the winner.
