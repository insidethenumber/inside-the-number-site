1 · THE FREE PICK

Kansas +4.5 vs Missouri (-110), 8:00 PM ET, David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium.

Missouri and Kansas play the Border War for the first time since 2011, and the number has traveled further than anything else on tonight's board. Missouri opened -7. It is -4.5 now.

Two and a half points is a large move in college football, and it matters more than its size suggests because of where it landed. It crossed 7, 6 and 5.5 on the way down — three of the most common final margins in the sport. A team laying 7 and a team laying 4.5 are being asked to win two different games.

The moneyline moved with it, -238 to -205, and the total fell the same direction, 53.5 to 51.5. That combination is the part worth reading. When the spread comes down and the total comes down together, the market is not shaving a number to balance tickets — it is lowering its estimate of the favorite and of the game.

Strip the book's cut out of the moneyline and the market now gives Kansas a 35.5% chance to win the game outright, up from roughly 33% when it opened. At +4.5, you are being paid for a number the market itself has been backing away from all week.

What beats it: Missouri covering the exact number everyone just walked away from. Line movement tells you where money went, not who wins — and a home dog in a rivalry game is precisely the spot where a market can overcorrect.

A number moving down while the price on that same side also moves down is the cleanest signal a total gives you — the money and the number agree. Most total moves have one going each way.

4 · WHAT THE MARKET KNOWS

Here is a thing worth knowing, from a game most people will never look at. Texas at Arizona opened with Arizona favored — Texas +109 on the moneyline, +1.5 on the run line. Tonight Texas is -104 and the run line has flipped to -1.5. The favorite and the underdog changed places. When that happens it is almost never the teams that changed; it is information about who is pitching, who is available, or who is resting. A flipped line is the market telling you it learned something after it opened. Worth checking what, before you take either side.
