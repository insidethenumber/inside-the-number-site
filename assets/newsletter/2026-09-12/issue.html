Under 49.5 — Ohio State at Texas (-110), 6:30 PM CT / 7:30 PM ET, ABC.

2 · BIGGEST OVERNIGHT MOVE

UCF at Pittsburgh opened at 51.5 and is now 55.5 — the largest total move anywhere on Saturday's board — while the over price crept just two cents, -108 to -110. A four-point move on a nearly frozen price usually means the number is catching up to something the market already knew, not chasing new money in real time. If the cause is public by kickoff — a change at quarterback, a wind report — it'll be visible before the 3:30 PM ET snap; if not, the honest read is that the number moved and the reason never surfaced.

3 · THE NUMBER TO KNOW

41.5 — Iowa State at Iowa carries the lowest total on Saturday's board, and it hasn't moved an inch all week — 41.5 at the open, 41.5 now. The under price has, though: -110 to -118. When a total is already at the floor, the market stops moving the number and starts moving the price instead.

4 · WHAT THE MARKET KNOWS

Eight cents of under money on a total that never moved is the same signal as a two-point line move somewhere else — it's just showing up in the only place left for it to show up. When a number is capped, at the floor, at a round total, or anywhere the book won't cross, the price becomes the only place left to watch. Reading it is the same skill either way: which side just got more expensive to bet, and by how much.
