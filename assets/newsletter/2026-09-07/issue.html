3 · THE NUMBER TO KNOW

14 — That's how many cents Detroit's moneyline loosened overnight, from -134 to -120, while its run line flipped a full point to underdog. When the price and the line move the same direction, the market is telling a consistent story -- less margin expected, not less chance to win.

4 · WHAT THE MARKET KNOWS

A spread and a moneyline moving in what look like opposite directions usually aren't disagreeing with each other. Detroit's moneyline got shorter (less negative) while its run line flipped against it -- that's the market getting less confident in a big Tigers win, not more confident in a Twins upset. Read a total the same way: when it drops a full run overnight, the way Cleveland-Baltimore's did, it's almost always new information about who's on the mound, not a sudden loss of faith in either lineup.
