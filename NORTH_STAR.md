## What the investigation found (Sep 2)

1. **Search is the channel that scales for sites like this.** Dimers.com does
   ~190K visits/month, 50% from organic search, on "[Team] vs [Team]
   prediction" pages. PickDawgz (a small shop) publishes one prediction article
   per game per day with that exact title format. Our /g/ pages were titled
   "Odds & True Price" — the wrong words. Fixed Sep 2: every game page is now
   "[City Team] vs [City Team] Prediction, Picks & Odds — date" with a
   prediction block and FAQ schema. Search takes 2-4 months to pay; it is the
   only compounding channel we have.
2. **X rewards replies, not broadcasts, and new accounts are throttled.** X
   assigns a reputation score; below the threshold only ~3 posts a day are even
   considered for distribution. Our original posts get 10-30 views. Replies
   under 100K-view posts are the only thing reaching strangers. Keep originals
   to 3/day, spend the rest on replies and quote posts.
3. **The small operators who actually grew did it with short video.**
   PickDawgz: 480K TikTok, 272K YouTube, 48K Instagram, and their site rides on
   that audience. Text-only newsletters in this niche grow slowly. This needs a
   decision from Chuck: a 60-second daily pick video (phone, one take) is the
   single biggest lever available and the only one we have not touched.
4. **No public track record means no reason to trust the picks.** Every
   guide and every competitor page leads with a record. Ours is private and
   currently losing. Until picks are winning, sell the *analysis* (true price,
   line movement) not the picks.
5. **Money needs affiliate links, and we have none.** Task #102 (Caesars,
   BetMGM applications) is the only path to revenue before paid subs exist.
