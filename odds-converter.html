What implied probability means

Every price is a probability wearing a costume. -110 means the book is charging you as if the outcome happens 52.4% of the time; +250 implies about 28.6%. Converting odds into a percentage is the fastest way to ask the only question that matters: does this happen more often than the price says?

One catch — implied probabilities on both sides of a game add up to more than 100%. The excess is the book's margin. To see prices with that margin stripped out, every game on today's board shows the no-vig win chance next to the posted price.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert American odds to decimal?

For positive odds: decimal = 1 + odds/100, so +150 becomes 2.50. For negative odds: decimal = 1 + 100/|odds|, so -110 becomes 1.909.

What is implied probability?

The win rate at which the bet breaks even: 1 divided by decimal odds. -110 implies 52.4%; +200 implies 33.3%.

Why do both sides of a game add to more than 100%?

The gap above 100% is the sportsbook's margin — often called vig or hold. It is the built-in cost of the bet.
